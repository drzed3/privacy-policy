Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Calculator application ("Calculator", "the App", or "we") handles user information.

1. Information We Collect

Calculator does not collect, store, or transmit personal information.

The App does not require users to create an account or provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, home address, or other contact information.

2. Use of the App

Calculator is a simple calculator application designed to perform mathematical calculations.

Calculations are processed within the App and are not sent to external servers.

3. Data Storage

Calculator does not maintain a personal user database or store personal information on external servers.

If the App provides a calculation history feature, that information is stored locally on the user's device and is not transmitted to the developer.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share users' personal information with third parties.

5. Advertising and Analytics

Calculator does not use advertising services or analytics services to collect personal information from users.

6. Children's Privacy

Calculator is designed to be suitable for users of all ages, including children.

The App does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Children can use the basic calculator functionality without creating an account or providing personal information.

7. Permissions

Calculator only uses the permissions necessary for the normal operation of the application.

The App does not request access to contacts, photos, messages, location, microphone, camera, or other personal information for its basic calculator functionality.

8. Security

Because Calculator does not collect or transmit personal information, there is no personal user database maintained by the developer.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated from time to time if the App's features or data practices change.

Any changes will be published on this page, together with an updated "Last Updated" date.

10. Contact

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy or the Calculator application, you may contact the developer, xlr8, through the contact information provided on the application's official distribution page.
