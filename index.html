Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Calculator app ("the App") handles information when you use the App.

Privacy First: The Calculator app is designed to be simple, safe, and privacy-friendly. The App does not require you to create an account or provide personal information to use its basic calculator features.

1. Information We Collect

The Calculator app does not require users to provide personal information such as their name, email address, phone number, home address, or account information.

The App does not intentionally collect sensitive personal information.

2. Calculator History

If the App provides a calculation history feature, calculation results may be stored locally on your device so that you can view them later.

This information is stored on your device and is not intentionally transmitted to us.

3. How We Use Information

The App uses information only when necessary to provide its calculator features and improve the user experience.

We do not use your personal information for advertising, selling, profiling, or other unrelated purposes.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or trade users' personal information.

We do not intentionally share personal information with third parties.

5. Internet Connection

The basic calculator functionality is designed to work without requiring users to create an online account.

If future versions of the App use third-party services, this Privacy Policy will be updated to explain what information those services may collect and how it is used.

6. Children's Privacy

The Calculator app is designed to be suitable for users of all ages, including children.

The App does not require children to provide personal information in order to use the basic calculator functionality.

We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Parents and guardians can allow children to use the App without requiring them to create an account or provide personal contact information.

7. Data Security

We take reasonable measures to protect information handled by the App. Because the App is designed to minimize data collection, there is limited personal information to protect.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Since the App does not require an account or collect personal information for its basic functionality, there is generally no personal account data that we need to retain.

If calculation history is stored locally on your device, you can delete it using the available features in the App or by removing the App from your device.

9. Third-Party Services

The App does not intentionally share users' personal information with third-party services.

If third-party services such as analytics, advertising, crash reporting, or other services are added in the future, this Privacy Policy will be updated accordingly.

10. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be published on this page with an updated "Last Updated" date.

11. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns about this Privacy Policy, you can contact us using the contact information provided on the application's Google Play listing.
